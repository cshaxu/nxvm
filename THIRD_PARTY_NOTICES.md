binary-release manifest and does not authorize a release. A release must also
produce the manifest and complete the checklist in `docs/release/`.

## Shipped Third-Party Components

None are currently approved for a release package. Third-party projects,
firmware, guest media, research tools, Microsoft files, and toolchain runtime
components are not covered by this notice set. A future release may include an
external component only after its license, notice text, source provenance,
redistributability, packaging role, and binary dependency have been recorded
in the release manifest.
