# M5 T199: Request Transport Egress Removal

Status: complete.

## Objective

Remove the unconsumed `vm_platform_request_transport` egress queue. The
transport remains the session-owned host-input ingress boundary; worker
lifecycle reports remain exclusively on the session-owned platform run handle.

## Scope

- Delete egress storage, API, and test-only egress coverage.
- Preserve ingress enqueue/dequeue, close/discard, consumer, and command-boundary
  semantics.
- Add a static closure gate rejecting egress symbols and stale T194 transport
  cancellation wording.
- Advance the runnable artifact to `0.5.0199`.

## Non-Goals

- Do not alter keyboard-state timing; T200 owns that behavior change.
- Do not alter worker reporting, cancellation, join, finalization, Console,
  debugger, guest startup, or media behavior.
- Do not add a replacement event queue. A future product-specific session-event
  contract requires a real producer and consumer before admission.

## Rules And Stop Conditions

- Apply single owner, worker-report-only, composition-only integration, wrapper
  discipline, and retained-NXVM behavior rules.
- Stop on a second cancellation route, ingress ordering change, run-handle
  regression, Console/debugger UX change, or FDD DOS-prompt failure.

## Required Evidence

- Focused transport smoke proves ingress consumer, close, and discard behavior.
- Static gate proves no egress API/storage remains and the T194 record names
  run-handle reporting as the worker cancellation boundary.
- Current CTest gate, retained Console lifecycle, two-session, run-handle, and
  FDD DOS-prompt smoke coverage pass.
- `nxvm_0_5_0199.exe` prints its matching banner and accepts `EXIT`.
