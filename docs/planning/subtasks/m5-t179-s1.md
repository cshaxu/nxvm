# M5 T179 S1: Direct CMOS And VM-Debug Ownership

## Scope

Make `cmos` and VM execution-debug state direct `vm_session` members. Update
the focused authority smokes and every composition caller to pass their
addresses where device APIs require pointers.

## Acceptance

- No `cmos_storage`, `debug_storage`, `cmos` alias pointer, or `debug` alias
  pointer remains in `vm_session`.
- Reset, pause/trace/record ownership remains session-local.
- CMOS and VM-debug authority gates pass without a Console/debugger workflow
  change.
