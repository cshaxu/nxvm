# M4 T4 S1: Freeze M5 Breakdown And Verification

## Objective

Turn the approved M4 ownership, firmware, and NXVM Console contracts into a
bounded M5 task/subtask breakdown with a regression owner, exact acceptance
evidence, artifact rule, and stop condition for every task.

## Non-Goals

- Do not begin M5 source migration or create an M5 executable.
- Do not weaken M1 fixture/watchdog evidence or claim guest completion.
- Do not add DOS, ntvdm64 CLI, Windows integration research, or guest media.

## Reference And Evidence

- M4 requirements: `docs/requirements/firmware-nxvm.md`.
- M1 behavior: `docs/verification/m1-t2-s1-nxvm-baseline.md`.
- M3 closure: `docs/verification/m3-t5-s1-closure.md`.

## Deliverables

- `docs/planning/m5-firmware-nxvm-implementation-breakdown.md`.
- M4 T4 tracking and M4 closure verification records.

## Verification

```text

rg -n "DOS|ntvdm64 run|Microsoft" docs/planning/m5-firmware-nxvm-implementation-breakdown.md
```

## Stop Conditions

Stop and escalate if any M5 task lacks a bounded owner or regression, requires
protected media, expands BIOS compatibility without a corpus requirement, or
absorbs M6/M8/M11 scope.
