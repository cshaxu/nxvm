# M5 T70 S2: FDC Boot Regression Repair

Status: complete.

## Incident

The T70 `0.5.0070` artifact can keep its execution loop alive after retained
Console `device fdd insert <image>` then `start`, but it does not boot the
fixture: the display remains a blinking cursor rather than reaching the DOS
prompt. It is revoked pending this repair.

## Cause And Repair Boundary

T70 connected FDC dependencies after `vm_machine_fdc_initialize`. That
initializer requires its port and DMA links and returned before registering the
FDC when they were absent. Bind the existing FDD, DMA, PIC, and port objects
immediately before FDC initialization; do not change their identity, the POST
order, FDC command semantics, or retained Console behavior.

## Required Evidence

- `nxvm-vm-dos-prompt-smoke <fdd image>` starts the full PC and observes an
  `X:\\>` prompt in the guest `B8000` text buffer within 3000 milliseconds.
- The worker must stop and join cleanly after that observation.
- The focused FDC authority and full-PC closure gates still pass.
- Rebuild and replace only `build/output/nxvm_0_5_0070.exe`; record its new
  SHA-256 as the repaired task artifact.

## Stop Conditions

Do not resume T71 until the prompt gate passes. A live process, blinking
cursor, or Console command acceptance is not boot evidence.

## Evidence

The FDC now receives its existing non-owning FDD, DMA, PIC, and port links
immediately before `vm_machine_fdc_initialize`, so it registers the same port
and DMA callbacks that the retained full PC requires. The focused FDC authority
and full-PC closure smoke tests pass. `nxvm-vm-dos-prompt-smoke D:\\fdd.img`
also passes, observing `A:\\>` in the guest B8000 text buffer within the
three-second budget.

The replacement `build/output/nxvm_0_5_0070.exe` has SHA-256
`D696905CD556303A2DFC93381AA93E5670804958F735A095CCDC0046BCBAEADA`.
