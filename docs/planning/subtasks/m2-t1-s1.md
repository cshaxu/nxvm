# M2 T1 S1: Design The Machine And DOS Architecture

## Objective

Produce the M2 design deliverables in
`docs/requirements/m2-machine-dos-architecture.md` from the verified M1
baseline without changing runtime behavior.

## Non-Goals

Do not refactor the baseline, implement DOS services, add Win32 platform code,
or turn an unresolved device observation into a product feature.

## Entry Conditions

1. M1 has a pinned source import, fixture evidence, and snapshot.
2. Each design question names its M1 observation or other evidence source.

## Expected Surface

- versioned Machine/DOS contracts and ownership/state diagrams;
- minimum-device and removal decisions;
- host-service trust-boundary requirements; and
- a behavior-preserving M3 migration and test plan; and
- the complete M3 Task/subtask breakdown required before M3 can start.

## Verification

- architecture review against the M1 trace/evidence records;
- interface review proves no forbidden dependency is introduced;
- a focused M3 checkpoint and regression plan; and
- updated tracking and verification records.

## Stop Conditions

Stop for an unbounded device investigation, a required undocumented Windows
mechanism, or a proposed DOS feature. Record the evidence question instead.
