# M5 T108 S1: C-Library Facade Audit

Status: complete.

## Objective

Audit every active C standard-library header, type, function, and macro use;
define the collision-safe all-uppercase `type.*` facade; and identify calls
that require a behavior decision rather than a mechanical alias.

## Non-Goals

- Do not change C code, standard-library call sites, or product behavior.
- Preserve the existing intentional uppercase wrapper names.
- Do not wrap platform APIs such as Win32, POSIX, pthread, curses, or tchar.

## Result

The inventory and approved migration rules are in
`docs/architecture/c-library-facade.md`. T108 is governance-only and produces
no executable.
