# M5 T13 S1: Composition Cutover Audit

## Objective

Inventory the remaining `baseline_*` composition names and direct legacy-owner
dependencies after T12 source-root closure. Define the smallest behavior-neutral
cutover sequence for the final `vm/main.c` product composition.

## Non-Goals

Do not rename or replace composition code, change Console/debugger behavior,
alter boot/media behavior, or start M6.

## Verification

Record each remaining dependency, its owner, its required compatibility gate,
and whether it is a name-only historical marker or an execution dependency.

## Stop Condition

Stop before any change that would alter the retained Console, debugger, startup,
media, or full-PC lifecycle without separate owner approval.
