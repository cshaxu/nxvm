## Completed Shape

- The runnable NXVM path is organized under `src/core/` and `src/vm/` with a
  non-runnable VDM skeleton under `src/vdm/`; root composition is product-owned.
- Core owns the synchronous executor, CPU/RAM/port bus, shared devices, and
  frozen lifecycle. VM owns full-PC devices, firmware/profile behavior, host
  integration, Console UX, and composition.
- The retained NXVM Console, debugger, boot path, FDD/HDD probes, session
  isolation, and text display/input route were preserved through the migration.
- CPU/FPU capability is explicit; default `80386 + no FPU` consumes x87 escape
  forms without misclassifying legal escapes as `#UD`. Complete 80386 and FPU
  emulation remain deferred.
- The completed PC-compatible slices are 8254 read-back, bounded 8042/KBC
  keyboard routing, and CGA text-controller state. Graphics, complete PIT/KBC
  behavior, native POSIX validation, and DOS execution remain deferred.
