# M5 Final L3 And Windows Closure Audit

## Purpose

Make the final M5 decision only after L3 hardware evidence and both Windows
3.1 lifecycle corpora are complete.

## Required scope

Review every M5 proposal, task history, device ledger, timing source decision,
artifact, and BYOB result.  Prove or reject the two M5 outcomes separately:
the selected PC/AT profile has source-backed L3 timing for its declared device
corpus, and Windows 3.1 Standard plus 386 Enhanced Mode install, start, and
shut down normally through the approved harness.  Transfer all unsupported
profiles/devices/compatibility breadth explicitly.

## Non-goals and stop conditions

No new implementation, M6 admission, broader Windows version, or release
claim.  A missing proof or reproduced defect returns to its earliest owner;
the audit may not waive it.

## Evidence standard

Require a requirement-to-evidence closure matrix, independent rerun of the
approved gates and lifecycle corpus, artifact/profile identity, residual debt
ledger, and an owner-visible M5 close/not-close decision.
