# IBM PC/AT 5170 Model-339 L3 Re-Audit

## Purpose

Repeat the final ready/not-ready audit for the frozen IBM PC/AT 5170 Model
339/Type 3 baseline after the ordered preclosure input/global-gate repair
passes. It must independently replay the T377 matrix rather than inherit
T377's negative decision or accept a repair from its focused tests alone.

## Scope and non-goals

The scope remains 8 MHz 80286, Rev.3 BIOS slot, 512 KB planar RAM, CGA, AT
keyboard and no fixed disk; the named 1.44 MB route remains aftermarket only.
No generic AT/clone, MFM/ATA, physical waveform, Windows, ROM/media import or
repair implementation is allowed.

## Evidence and completion

Require fresh independent requirement/component matrices, native-input/reset/
cancellation and cross-device replay, focused Model-339/FDC proof and a
passing current gate. Every selected capability must be functionally complete
with source-backed or explicitly bounded deterministic L3 timing; each
physical or out-of-baseline boundary remains visible. It makes the only later
Model-339 L3 ready/not-ready decision.
