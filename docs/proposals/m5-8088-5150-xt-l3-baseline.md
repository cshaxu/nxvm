# IBM PC/XT 5160-268 Board And Device Phase-Timing Closure

## Purpose

Close board-local availability, device service, and phase timing for the
already selected IBM PC/XT 5160-268 after its 8088 CPU, profile/capability, and
selected-device functional candidates close. It precedes the separate XT final
L3 audit and is an 8088 physical-machine timing contract, not an 8086 hardware
claim.

## Required scope

Consume the closed 8088 CPU, 5160-268 profile, and functionally complete device
results, then map the selected 8088 CPU, memory/ROM, ISA, 8237 DMA, 8259 PIC,
PIT, keyboard, display, storage and NMI/relevant board paths to one
source-labelled availability, reset, publication, and trace contract. Use
IBM/Intel primary documentation and project-owned probes; where it gives a
range or no exact value, named 86Box/MAME/PCjs comparisons may be non-imported,
profile-local cross-checks under the source policy.

The accepted Xebec fixed-disk route is included: its DMA3/IRQ5 availability,
request/response publication, reset/cancellation ordering and any
source-proven service phase belong to this machine-local contract. Missing
mechanical, rotational or transfer-time evidence remains explicitly bounded;
no guessed deadline may make the logical route appear physical.

## Non-goals and stop conditions

No 8086 substitution, unclosed prerequisite, XT/PC hybrid, device functional
repair, universal ISA timing, copied emulator code, protected-mode claim,
Windows claim, host-time coupling or firmware/media import. Stop if the exact
board/manual/corpus cannot identify an owner or a timing value; retain the
receiver rather than borrowing PC/AT behavior.

## Evidence standard

Require 5160-268 board/profile provenance, a CPU/device/bus/NMI route ledger,
source-to-model or reference-exhausted timing table, contention and reset
replay, project-owned firmware/DOS probes, full current-gate evidence, and a
complete handoff to the final XT audit.
