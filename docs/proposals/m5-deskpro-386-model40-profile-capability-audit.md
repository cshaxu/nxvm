# DeskPro 386 Model 40 Profile And Capability-Gap Audit

## Purpose

Lock the 1986 original Compaq DeskPro 386 Model 40 (DeskPro 386/16) as the
single 80386 baseline and identify its required capability gaps before CPU,
device-function, or board-timing work begins. This candidate follows the final
5170 audit and is planning/audit work only.

## Required scope

Use Compaq primary material to establish the exact model/revision, 16 MHz
80386 configuration, memory/ROM constraints, selected display, input, storage,
interrupt/DMA, timer, NMI, reset, and project-owned corpus. Map those selected
components against the frozen shared device ledger, recording functional and
timing gaps, earliest owner, and the proper later DeskPro receiver. The required
BIOS slot is an external owner-managed research asset; the repository records
no vendor bytes, local path, or catalogue.

## Non-goals and stop conditions

No CPU repair, device implementation, board-timing allocation, generic 386-PC
or later DeskPro inference, Windows claim, ROM/media import, or L3 decision.
Stop where source material cannot identify the selected hardware and leave an
explicit evidence/probe receiver rather than silently choosing a clone value.

## Evidence standard

Require source-labelled profile provenance, a Model-40 bill of materials, a
component/capability-gap matrix, and a handoff that separates CPU, shared
functional, and DeskPro-local timing ownership.
