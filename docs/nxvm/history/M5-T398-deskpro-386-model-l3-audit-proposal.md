# DeskPro 386 Model 40 Model-L3 Closure Audit

## Purpose

Audit the exact 1986 original DeskPro 386 Model 40 (DeskPro 386/16) only after
its profile/capability, CPU, selected-device functional, and board/device
candidates close. This fifth DeskPro candidate makes the ready/not-ready
decision and never absorbs missing implementation.

## Required scope

Reconcile the chosen Model-40 configuration's CPU/state, memory/I/O
availability, DMA, interrupt/NMI, device service, reset, cancellation and
deterministic ordering evidence. Confirm every selected component is
functionally complete and has a source-backed or explicitly model-derived L3
contract; every reference-exhausted boundary has one receiver. Distinguish the
result from both 5170 and PC/XT 5160-268.

## Non-goals and stop conditions

No repair work, expanded DeskPro variant, Windows lifecycle claim, x87 numeric
claim, physical cycle-exact claim or generic 80386-PC conclusion. Any defect
returns to the earlier profile, CPU, device-functional, or board/device
candidate.

## Evidence standard

Require an independent requirement-to-evidence matrix, cross-device replay,
current-gate rerun and an owner-visible DeskPro model-L3 ready/not-ready
decision.
